### Scoring model
Weighted scorecard, each category scored `0-5` and converted to weighted contribution:
- Architecture & separation of concerns: `25%`
- Testability & automated quality gates: `20%`
- Reusability/modularity: `15%`
- Correctness & robustness: `15%`
- Scalability for feature growth: `15%`
- Build/dependency/operational hygiene: `10%`

### Severity model
- Critical: immediate breakage/data-loss/security-risk level issue.
- High: substantial architecture/correctness risk likely to block near-term scale.
- Medium: maintainability/testability drag with moderate delivery impact.
- Low: improvement opportunity with limited short-term impact.

### Review process quality checks
- Completeness: each scorecard category includes 3+ evidence points.
- Traceability: each risk entry includes exact file/line or command evidence.
- Reproducibility: all baseline checks are rerunnable from repo root.
- Decision integrity: go/no-go conclusion is derived directly from risk severities.
